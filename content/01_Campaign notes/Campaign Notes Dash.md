## Welcome to the City of Roxton

You are a team of explorers who have been hired to aid the Historical Society of Roxton. Roxton is a bustling city deep in its scientific advancement era. The Society is the head of this advancement. Your group being chosen is an enormous honor.

The City of Roxton is booming in size as houses go up over top of each other. In the center of it all is The Historical Society. They own a tall building connected to two academies. The Academy of the Spiritual and the Academy of Science and Technology. Rivals in nature blending together to advance all aspects of Roxton.
