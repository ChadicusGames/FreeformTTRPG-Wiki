### Level Up Attempts
To do this roll a d6 for each skill you failed at during the session so keep track of when you fail a roll. You will Roll the dice and if you get higher than your current skill level then you will go up a level. You will only get one attempt per skill no matter how many times you failed at it.

> you failed your cognitive skill in the fight and now you are unconscious. your current skill is 4. If your roll a 5 or 6 congrats your skill will go up to 5. IF you roll a 1- 4 then it stays the same. 

If at any point you get a skill to level 6, see [[4.5.2_Mastered Skills]].
