## The Location

From our tone and mantra we have developed an abstract idea of the world space we are playing it. Now lets get some specifics, create one place that the players will start in. Don't build an entire continent, just one place to start the story. 

> __The Unkempt Wilds__
> _A wilderness of mythical creatures living in harmony cut up by dirt paths of merchants and explorers. Shifting landscapes and tall plush grasses hide the danger with in from the passerby's. Adventuring off the trail is the only way to see its true unruly beauty._
